Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for this portfolio.

This policy explains how information is handled when you visit this portfolio or contact me through the website.

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Information This Website May Collect

This is a personal portfolio website. It does not require account registration and does not sell user data.

  • Contact form details: your name, email address, subject, and message if you submit the form.
  • Email communication details when you contact me directly by email.
  • Theme preference stored in your browser so dark or light mode can remain consistent.
  • Public GitHub contribution data displayed from my GitHub profile.

How The Information Is Used

  • To reply to your message or collaboration request.
  • To maintain the portfolio experience, including the theme preference.
  • To display portfolio-related public information such as projects, resume, certificates, and GitHub activity.

Third-Party Services

This website may use third-party services to provide normal portfolio features.

  • EmailJS may process contact form submissions so messages can reach my email inbox.
  • GitHub or a GitHub contribution API may be used to show public contribution activity.
  • CDN providers may load fonts, icons, or scripts used by the interface.
  • External links may take you to GitHub, LinkedIn, Facebook, or other project resources.

Cookies And Local Storage

This site does not intentionally use advertising cookies. It may use browser local storage only for simple preferences, such as remembering the selected theme.

Data Sharing

I do not sell or rent your personal information. Contact details are used only to communicate with you, unless disclosure is required by law or needed to protect the website from abuse.

Your Choices

  • You can avoid the contact form and email me directly instead.
  • You can clear local storage in your browser to remove saved theme preference.
  • You may request deletion of messages you sent by contacting me.
